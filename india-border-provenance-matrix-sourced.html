Two irreconcilable colonial notifications: August 1875 (Cachar's southern boundary, Assam Gazette, drawn under the Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation 1873 in consultation with Mizo chiefs) and March 1933 (Lushai Hills/Manipur boundary, drawn without Mizo consultation). Radcliffe, then six decades of correction.
